# SSEO privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

SSEO is a browser extension that audits the SEO of the web pages you choose and keeps a history of those audits on your computer. This policy covers what it stores, what it sends, and how to delete it. It is short because there is little to say.

## What SSEO stores

When you audit a page, SSEO saves this on your computer, inside your browser profile:

- the page address and its title
- the date of the audit and its scores
- the audit results: each check, its outcome, short excerpts of the page's HTML used as evidence (for example the page title or a meta tag), and measurements such as word count or HTML size

Nothing else. SSEO does not read your browsing history, your bookmarks, your cookies, or any page you have not asked it to audit.

## What SSEO sends, and where

SSEO has no server. There is no account and no analytics, and nothing you audit ever reaches the developer.

To run an audit, your browser requests, directly from the site being audited:

- the page itself
- that site's `robots.txt` and sitemap
- each link found on the page, to check whether it is broken (these can be on other sites)

These requests happen only when you start an audit, from the side panel or the dashboard. They carry no cookies. The site sees a logged-out visitor.

## Sharing

SSEO does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Ever. It is not used for advertising, for creditworthiness, or for anything other than showing you your own audits.

## Deleting your data

- Delete one page's audits, a whole site, or all history from the dashboard.
- Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.
